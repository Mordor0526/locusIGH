# locusIGH

Analysis scripts for the IgH locus in South American camelids.

## Citation

Peña-Monroy A, Alemán-Gutiérrez Á, Flores-Morales G, Valenzuela S, Marín JC. Genetic diversity and selection signatures at the immunoglobulin heavy-chain locus in South American camelids. *Animals* (submitted).

## Scripts

- `diversity_stats.py` — Nucleotide and haplotype diversity (π, Hd) by species and region
- `tajima_d_igh.py` — Tajima's D per gene and functional category
- `make_figures.py` — Figures 1, 4, 5, 6 (locus map, F_ST heatmap, Tajima's D plots)
- `plot_tree.py` — Figure 2 (ML phylogeny)
- `pca_plot.py` — Figure 3 (PCA)
- `diversity_by_gene.py` — Per-gene diversity statistics
- `diversity_global.py` — Global diversity statistics
